About Me

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Statistics at Peking University. My work lies at the intersection of operations research and artificial intelligence, with a focus on LLM systems, AI agents, and diffusion models.

I am particularly interested in building useful and reliable AI agents that support real-world research and decision-making, as well as applying operations research and mathematical modeling to formulate and solve problems arising in AI systems.
